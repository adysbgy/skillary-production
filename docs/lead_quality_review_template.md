# Lead Quality Review Template

This template is designed for the Skillary sales operations team to review the first 10-20 leads generated post-launch to assess traffic quality and intent.

## Signals of a Strong B2B Lead
- Uses a corporate/organization email address (not @gmail.com / @yahoo.com).
- Has a clear participant count (e.g., "Tim kami ada 15 orang").
- Mentions a specific training topic or area.
- Has a specific timeline (e.g., "Q3 tahun ini").
- Explicitly asks for a proposal or demo.
- Asks specific questions about reporting, assessment, or certificates.

### Lead Quality Categories
- **Hot:** Ready to buy, budget confirmed, immediate need.
- **Warm:** Valid B2B profile, exploring options, needs proposal/demo.
- **Nurture:** Valid profile but no immediate timeline, or individual looking for B2C courses (not currently offered).
- **Not Fit:** Spam, students, or completely irrelevant inquiries.
