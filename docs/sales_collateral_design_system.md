# Sales Collateral Design System

This document outlines the visual design system and claim-safety guidelines for producing Skillary sales collateral (Corporate Proposal, Sales Deck, Company Profile).

## 1. Color Palette
- **Deep Navy (`#172554` / `#1E3A8A`)**: Primary brand color for large headings, primary CTAs, and trust elements.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FFFDF9`)**: Primary page background. Provides a premium, warm, corporate feel.
- **Warm Cream / Linen (`#FAF3EA` / `#FFF8F1`)**: Secondary block backgrounds.
- **Skillary Amber (`#D88A44`)**: Action color, focus rings, hover states, and subtle accents.
- **Deep Orange (`#C2410C`)**: High-contrast text accent for links or eyebrow text.
- **Warm Border (`#E7DDD4`)**: Dividers, card borders, and subtle structural lines.
- **Text Dark (`#1F2937` / `#0F172A`)**: Primary reading text.
- **Muted Warm Text (`#6B625A` / `#475569`)**: Secondary text, subtitles, and disclaimers.

## 2. Typography Guidance
- **Primary Font**: Professional sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Roboto, or standard system fonts).
- **Headings**: Large, bold, tightly tracked.
- **Body Text**: Generous line height (1.6+), legible sizes.
- **Spacing**: Use generous whitespace. Do not clutter slides or pages.

## 3. Visual Style (Do's)
- **Warm Premium Corporate**: The look should feel established, clean, and trustworthy.
- **Clean Cards**: Use subtle borders (`#E7DDD4`) and white (`#FFFFFF`) cards on the ivory background to structure information.
- **Restrained Icons**: Use simple, modern, geometric icons.
- **Mockups**: Use clean abstractions of dashboards or certificates as illustrations.

## 4. Forbidden Visual Style (Don'ts)
- ❌ **Cheap Promo Badges**: No "Buy Now", "Diskon 50%", or flashy red starbursts.
- ❌ **Fake Client Logo Wall**: No "Trusted By" grids containing unauthorized logos.
- ❌ **Playful Emojis**: Avoid excessive emojis in formal PDFs or Decks.
- ❌ **Loud Gradients**: Avoid harsh neon or complex gradients. Stick to flat colors or very subtle linear washes.
- ❌ **Cluttered Slides**: One core message per slide.

## 5. Claim-Safety Design Rules
1. **No Client Logos**: Unless explicit written permission is granted, use text-based sector references (e.g., "Sektor Perbankan").
2. **No Fake Testimonials**: Do not invent quotes or attach fake headshots.
3. **No ROI Inventions**: Do not fabricate numbers like "Increased productivity by 300%".
4. **Mandatory Legacy Disclaimer**: Any page referencing the legacy portfolio must include the following text in small font at the bottom:
   > *"Portofolio ini disusun dari arsip dokumentasi pelatihan Allman dan digunakan sebagai referensi pengalaman yang melatarbelakangi pengembangan Skillary. Penggunaan nama, logo, atau dokumentasi institusi mengikuti izin publikasi dan validasi internal."*
