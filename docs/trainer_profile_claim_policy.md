# Trainer Profile Claim Policy

- Public profiles require recorded trainer consent and final preview approval.
- `Selected` means curated for a Skillary context; it is not certification.
- `Verified` requires identity review, relevant experience review, and at least one supporting evidence item.
- Metrics require an internal source note and reproducible calculation.
- Organization names describe context only and must not imply active endorsement.
- Credentials must include issuer and status; verification links are preferred.
- Testimonials require participant context and publication consent.
- Profiles can be corrected, suspended, or removed when evidence becomes invalid.
- Personal phone numbers, private email, identity scans, and internal consent references are never published.
